## Start here: free with your Rutgers account

Rutgers pays for these, so you don't need a personal subscription. When you sign in with your Rutgers account, Rutgers says your chats are not used to train the vendors' models. Free personal accounts don't always give you that protection.

| Tool | Good for | Sign in |
|:-----|:---------|:--------|
| [Microsoft Copilot Chat](https://it.rutgers.edu/ai/microsoft-copilot/) | Questions, summaries, drafting, code help, images | [copilot.cloud.microsoft](https://copilot.cloud.microsoft/) with your Rutgers email and NetID password |
| [Google Gemini](https://it.rutgers.edu/ai/google-gemini/) | Writing, brainstorming, planning, and LearnLM study plans | [gemini.google.com](https://gemini.google.com/) with your ScarletMail account |
| [Gemini Notebook](https://it.rutgers.edu/ai/gemini-notebook/) (formerly NotebookLM) | Asking questions about your own readings and notes, with citations | [notebook.google.com](https://notebook.google.com/) with your ScarletMail account |

More Rutgers-provided tools, research computing, and campus programs are on [Rutgers tools](rutgers-tools.md).

## Before you use AI for a class

{: .important }
AI use in coursework is not allowed unless your instructor clearly permits it. Rules differ by course, so check the syllabus or ask. ([OIT guidance](https://it.rutgers.edu/ai/guidance-on-the-use-of-ai-at-rutgers/))

- Don't enter confidential information, protected health information (PHI), or other proprietary Rutgers information into AI tools.
- Check every fact, quote, and citation an AI gives you. Models can invent sources that don't exist.
- When AI use is allowed, disclose it the way your instructor asks and cite it in your course's citation style.

Details and links are on [Using AI responsibly](responsible-use.md).
