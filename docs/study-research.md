## Studying your own course material

- [Gemini Notebook](https://it.rutgers.edu/ai/gemini-notebook/) (formerly NotebookLM) is free with ScarletMail. Upload lecture slides, readings, or notes (only material your instructor allows you to share), then ask questions and get answers that cite your sources. It can also turn sources into study guides, flashcards, quizzes, mind maps, and audio overviews. See the [Gemini Notebook tutorial](tutorials/notebooklm.md).
- [Google Gemini](https://it.rutgers.edu/ai/google-gemini/) and [Microsoft Copilot Chat](https://it.rutgers.edu/ai/microsoft-copilot/) are free with your Rutgers account. Use them to explain a concept several ways, quiz you, or check your reasoning.

{: .note }
AI is most useful for studying when it makes you do the work. Ask it to quiz you or explain where your answer went wrong, not to produce the answer.

## Finding and reading research

| Tool | What it does | Cost |
|:-----|:-------------|:-----|
| [Rutgers Libraries databases](https://libguides.rutgers.edu/ai-eresource-platforms) | Scopus, Web of Science, JSTOR, ProQuest, EBSCO, and other databases the Libraries license now have built-in AI features. This guide explains what each one does. | Free through Rutgers |
| [Semantic Scholar](https://www.semanticscholar.org/) | Free AI-powered search across the scientific literature, run by the nonprofit Ai2 | Free |
| [Elicit](https://elicit.com/) | Searches more than 138 million papers, summarizes them, and lets you chat with a paper's full text | Free Basic plan |
| [Consensus](https://consensus.app/) | AI search engine that answers questions from peer-reviewed literature | Free plan |
| [Perplexity](https://www.perplexity.ai/) | Web search that answers in prose with linked sources | Free plan. Discounted [Education Pro](student-offers.md). |

{: .warning }
AI research tools miss important papers, mix up findings, and sometimes cite papers that don't exist. Open and read every source before you cite it. For a thorough or systematic search, use the library databases and [ask a Rutgers librarian](https://www.libraries.rutgers.edu/).

## Writing

- [Grammarly for Education](https://it.rutgers.edu/grammarly/) is provided by Rutgers and covers grammar and style feedback plus AI brainstorming and revision help.
- Course rules on AI in writing vary a lot. Some instructors allow grammar fixes but not AI-written drafts. Check before you use AI on graded writing, and see [citing AI](responsible-use.md#citing-ai).
