# Student offers
{: .no_toc }

Free and discounted plans for verified students, checked against each vendor's own page on the date at the bottom of this page. Offers change often, so confirm the terms before you sign up.

{: .warning }
These are personal accounts, not Rutgers accounts, so Rutgers' data protections don't apply to them. In October 2026, OIT [warned faculty and staff](https://it.rutgers.edu/2026/10/15/important-free-or-discounted-ai-offers-and-rutgers-data/) that free and discounted vendor offers "almost certainly" lack the privacy and security protections of Rutgers-approved tools. Use these plans for personal learning. For Rutgers work, use the [Rutgers tools](rutgers-tools.md). Most free trials also need a payment card and renew at full price unless you cancel.

## Time-limited promotions

Some vendors run seasonal promotions instead of standing student plans. Old blog posts often advertise offers that have already ended.

- **ChatGPT Plus:** OpenAI's [fall 2026 offer for U.S. students](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001493-chatgpt-back-to-school-offer-for-students) has a claim deadline of **October 31, 2026**.
- **Cursor:** The free year of Cursor Pro for students is no longer listed. [Cursor's student page](https://cursor.com/students) now points to promotions at campus and online events.
- **Colab Pro:** The free year of Colab Pro for U.S. university students is ["no longer available for new signups"](https://research.google.com/colaboratory/faq.html).

No verification needed. Free plans have lower usage limits and may not include the newest models.

| Tool | Free plan |

| [Google Colab](https://colab.research.google.com/) and [Kaggle](https://www.kaggle.com/code) | Free notebooks with limited GPU time. See [Coding and data](coding.md#free-gpus-and-notebooks). |
